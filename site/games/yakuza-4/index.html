Main Story

Four Parts, one per protagonist (Akiyama, Saejima, Tanimura, Kiryu), then the Finale. "Heir to the Legend" needs a full replay on Legend, only unlocked after beating Hard — New Game+ is fine, but no Game Over retries on Legend, so save often. "Indomitable" means clearing the whole game on Normal without ever dropping to Easy.
