Main Story

Chapters 1–15 each award a Bronze trophy; Chapter 16 (the finale) completes the main story and awards "Thank You." (Silver). The Majima Saga is a separate self-contained bonus mode with its own trophy. "Dragon of Legend" needs a full playthrough on Legend difficulty — unlike Yakuza 6, you can't just switch difficulty on a late save; New Game+ works, though.
