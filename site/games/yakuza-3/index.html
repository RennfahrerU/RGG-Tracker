Main Story

Most chapters end in a boss fight with its own Bronze trophy — Chapters 9 and 11 don't have one. "Thank You!" fires after the full story (past Chapter 12). "Testament to Strength" needs a full replay on Legend difficulty, only unlocked after beating the game on Hard — New Game+ is fine, but you can't retry from a Game Over on Legend, so save often.
