Main Story

Even-numbered chapters (2–16) each award a Bronze trophy; the Finale awards "Awakened and Unleashed" (Silver). Eight more trophies are missable story moments — do them the first time through, they won't come back around. "Stuff of Legend" needs a full second playthrough on Legend difficulty after finishing the game once.
